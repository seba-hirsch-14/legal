Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how this application ("App") handles information when you use it. This App is a private tool built for personal use by its owner and is not intended for public use.

1. Information We Access

This App accesses data through the Meta Marketing API, including but not limited to: ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads, and performance insights. This data is accessed solely using the account owner's own Meta access token and pertains only to the owner's own Meta Ads accounts.

2. How We Use the Information

All data accessed is used exclusively for personal advertising management purposes. No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

3. Data Storage

This App does not store any Meta user data on external servers or databases. All data is retrieved in real time and displayed locally on the owner's device.

4. Data Sharing

This App does not share any data with third parties. It operates exclusively for the personal use of its owner.

5. Data Security

Access tokens and credentials are stored locally in environment variables and are never transmitted to third parties or stored in public repositories.

6. Cookies

This App does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

7. User Rights

As this App is intended for single-user personal use only, all data accessed belongs to the App owner. The owner retains full control over their data at all times through their Meta account settings.

8. Data Deletion

Since no user data is stored by this App, there is nothing to delete. If you wish to revoke the App's access to your Meta data, you may do so at any time through your Meta account settings under "Apps and Websites."

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the app owner directly.
