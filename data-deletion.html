Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: April 15, 2025

This application is a private personal tool that does not store any user data on external servers or databases. All data accessed through the Meta Marketing API is retrieved in real time and never persisted.

How to Revoke App Access

Since no data is stored by this App, there is nothing to delete from our side. However, if you wish to revoke this App's access to your Meta account, follow these steps:

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings
  2. Click on "Apps and Websites"
  3. Find this App in the list
  4. Click "Remove"

This will immediately revoke all access tokens associated with this App and no further data will be accessible.

Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding your data, please contact the app owner directly.
